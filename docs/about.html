YC
Yassine Chaikhi
Founder & Lead Editor, ExpatScore.de
Agadir, Morocco → Germany expert

When I first dug deep into the German financial system, I was overwhelmed. SCHUFA score, Anmeldung, blocked account, Steuer-ID, public vs. private health insurance — each of these concepts has its own jargon, its own rules and its own traps for newcomers.

The information I found online was either written in German for Germans — or in English, but hopelessly superficial. Nobody explained what a SCHUFA score means for someone who has just arrived and has no German financial history yet.

So I started figuring it out myself. For every question I could answer, I wrote down the answer. Those answers became ExpatScore.de.

"I'm building the resource I wish I'd had — detailed, honest, and written by someone who knows what that information gap feels like." — Yassine Chaikhi

Identity
What ExpatScore.de is — and what it isn't

ExpatScore.de is not a bank, not a broker and not a financial advisor. We are an independent editorial portal. That means:

✓
Comparisons, guides & tools: We create detailed content to help expats find their way around the German financial system.
✓
Transparent funding: We are funded through affiliate commissions — fully disclosed here.
✓
Only reputable recommendations: We don't recommend any product we don't consider reputable and suitable for expats.
✓
No paid recommendations: All content reflects our own research. No provider can buy a positive review.

Method
Our editorial standards

How we create content, and what sets our comparisons apart from other portals.

🔬
Our own research
Every comparison is based on public product information, price and terms lists, and our own product tests. No press releases.
🔄
Regular updates
Terms change. We review our content regularly and mark every article with its last update date.
🎯
Expat focus
We look at every product from a newcomer's perspective: language barriers, no SCHUFA history, visa restrictions.
⚖️
Independent editorial team
Providers have no influence on our reviews. We state the downsides clearly — including for affiliate partners.
📖
Plain language
SCHUFA, public health insurance, Anmeldung and blocked accounts — explained for someone who has never been to Germany. Without losing depth.
🌍
International perspective
Expats come from 180+ countries. We take differences in visa types, nationalities and residence status into account.
