What is N26? — Background 2026

N26 is a Berlin-founded digital bank that has held a full BaFin banking licence since 2016. With over 8 million customers in 24 European countries, N26 is one of the world's most successful neobanks. For expats in Germany, N26 is the first choice for one decisive reason: no SCHUFA score is required to open an account.

That means: you can literally open an account on the day you arrive in Germany — with just a passport, a smartphone and a working email address. VideoIdent takes 8 minutes. The virtual card is usable immediately, and the physical Mastercard arrives by post within 5–10 working days.

In 2026, N26 adjusted its product range slightly. The former "N26 You" plan was replaced by N26 Go, which offers travel insurance and 5 free ATM withdrawals per month in the eurozone (foreign-currency withdrawals free). The Instant Savings interest rate is linked to the ECB key rate — 2% p.a. for Metal customers as a new-customer offer, 1.50% p.a. regular (as of November 2026).

    N26 and SCHUFA: how to build your score

    This is the most important section for expats who have just arrived in Germany. N26 reports positive account data to SCHUFA. That means: every month you hold an active N26 account has a positive effect on your SCHUFA score — even if you don't owe a single euro.

    With the SCHUFA reform of 17 March 2026, the system became fundamentally more transparent: instead of 250+ unclear factors, only 12 clearly defined criteria are now assessed. The age of your oldest bank account is one of them — which makes opening an N26 account early more valuable than ever.

    Month 1 — Arrival day
    Open N26 Standard free. VideoIdent in 8 minutes. Virtual card usable immediately. Your SCHUFA clock starts ticking.
    Months 1–3 — Foundation
    Have your salary paid into your N26 account. Pay all bills (rent, electricity, phone) on time. No payment reminders — ever.
    Months 3–6 — First credit card
    Apply for a secured credit card (e.g. Barclays or Hanseatic Bank). Make 2–3 small purchases a month — always pay the balance back in full. Combined SCHUFA boost from account + credit card.
    Months 6–12 — Apply for DKB
    With 6 months of positive history you can now apply for a DKB or C24 account. Run both accounts side by side — your SCHUFA profile keeps growing.
    Months 12–18 — Target state
    Request your free SCHUFA data copy (meineSCHUFA.de, Art. 15 GDPR). Your score should now be in the "Good" to "Excellent" range (709–999). ING and Commerzbank are now within reach.

    Our conclusion: is N26 worth it in 2026?

    For expats who are new to Germany, N26 Standard is still the best first bank. The reason is simple: there is no alternative that is simultaneously free, instantly accessible, without a SCHUFA score and available in English — while also building SCHUFA history.

    The only significant downside is the new fee for the physical card (€10 one-off). That is bearable. Anyone who regularly needs cash can manage well with the N26 Standard plan and occasional CASH26 use.

    After 6–12 months, moving to DKB or ING makes sense — but N26 should be kept as a base account for international transfers and English-language everyday banking.
