⚠ Outdated figure — €934/month: The old requirement of €934 per month (€11,208 per year) expired in autumn 2024. Many websites and older checklists still show it, but German consulates reject blocked account confirmations based on the old amount. Always calculate with €992 (students) or €1,091 (Chancenkarte and other categories).

Transfer €100–200 more than the minimum. International transfers lose money along the way — intermediary bank fees and currency conversion mean the amount that actually arrives in your blocked account can be slightly less than what you sent. If even a few euros are missing, the provider cannot issue your blocking confirmation (Sperrbestätigung) until you top up. The surplus is not lost: it stays in your account and is paid out to you in Germany.

Why two different amounts?

There is no single blocked account amount for Germany. The Federal Foreign Office sets the required sum per visa category, based on the BAföG student support rate — and there are two different amounts. Students get the base rate; most other applicants must show the base rate plus 10%:

Visa categoryPer month12 months
Student visa (§16b)€992€11,904
Chancenkarte / Opportunity Card (§20a) — job seekers€1,091€13,092
Language course, qualification recognition & study-search visas€1,091€13,092

If your visa is valid for less than 12 months, the total scales with the number of months — for example €5,952 for a 6-month student stay (€992 × 6) or €6,546 for 6 months on the Chancenkarte (€1,091 × 6).

Frequently asked questions

How much do I need in a blocked account for a student visa?

€992 per month — €11,904 for a 12-month stay. This rate has applied since January 2025 and is unchanged for 2026.

How much do I need for the Chancenkarte (Opportunity Card)?

€1,091 per month — up to €13,092 for 12 months. That is the student base rate of €992 plus a 10% surcharge, so job seekers need €1,188 more than students for a full year.

Do I need a blocked account for a Blue Card?

No. Blue Card holders and skilled worker visa applicants prove financial support through their employment contract and salary offer instead. See our Blue Card Tool for the salary thresholds.
